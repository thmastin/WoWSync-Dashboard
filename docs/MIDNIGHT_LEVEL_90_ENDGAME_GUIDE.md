# World of Warcraft Retail Midnight 12.1 / Season 2 — Level 90 Endgame Guide

**Scope:** live Retail Midnight Patch 12.1 / Season 2, research snapshot 25 September 2026. This is a guide to understand the entire level-90 ecosystem before choosing a character-specific next action. It assumes you last played serious endgame around Cataclysm.

**Version rule:** do not mix this guide with The War Within, Midnight Season 1/12.0.5, Classic, or 12.1.5 PTR information. When the live UI conflicts with a guide, the live UI wins.

## 1. The model: endgame is several parallel lanes

Modern Retail does not have one mandatory “heroics → raid” line. It has overlapping systems:

| Lane | What it teaches/provides | Main cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign and outdoor world | unlocks, starter replacements, story, basic crests | mostly one-time plus daily/weekly events |
| Coiled Isle / Vaults of Atal'Utek | 12.1 outdoor events, currencies, Veteran rewards | introductory unlock plus recurring events |
| Prey | opt-in outdoor hunts, gear/crests/World Vault progress | gear-relevant first hunts per difficulty weekly |
| Delves | 10–15 minute solo-to-five scenarios | Bountiful locations rotate daily; keys are scarce |
| Heroic / Mythic 0 dungeons | learn group encounters, direct dungeon drops | M0 daily in current S2 |
| Mythic+ | timed escalating five-player dungeons | repeatable; no weekly instance lockout |
| Raid / Lair | group boss content, tier and encounter loot | weekly lockout rhythm |
| Crafting | planned weak-slot fixes and embellishments | Spark-gated, upgraded through recrafting |

The useful question is not “which activity is mandatory?” It is: **which enjoyable activity gives a direct upgrade, a useful weekly Vault option, practice for a desired mode, or an appropriately-tiered upgrade resource?**

## 2. Start at 90: unlock before you grind

For a first character, finish the main Midnight campaign and the 12.1 campaign/Coiled Isle entry. The Vaults of Atal'Utek introduction unlocks major activities for the Warband, so it has future-alt value. Enable Prey only if you want its outdoor-hunt loop; it is optional.

Do not farm old Season 1 world-boss drops as a gearing plan: 12.1 notes say some remain Season 1 and cannot be upgraded. Use obvious outdoor upgrades while progressing, but move on when Heroic, Bountiful Delve, M0, raid, or M+ rewards can replace the same slot.

## 3. Gear tracks and upgrades

An item’s **track** is its upgrade ceiling. Its present item level is not the whole story.

| Track | current range | common sources |
|---|---:|---|
| Adventurer | 269–282 | outdoor, Normal Prey, T1–4 Delves |
| Veteran | 282–295 | Heroic, LFR, Hard Prey, T5–6 Delves |
| Champion | 295–308 | M0, +2–3, Normal raid, Nightmare Prey, T7–10 Delves |
| Hero | 308–321 | Heroic raid/Lair, +4–8, high Delve routes |
| Myth | 321–334 | Mythic raid/Lair, +9+ |

Each track has six ranks. Rank 1 is free; later ranks use matching **Mistcrests** and gold. Same-slot catch-up can make lower upgrade steps free when that character has already earned a higher item level for the slot. Warband achievements can lower some crest costs for alts; this is a discount, not account-shared gear or Vault progress.

Spend a crest when an item is durable, completes a tier breakpoint, unlocks content you want to play, or has a unique useful effect. Hold it when a known Vault choice, craft, or guaranteed encounter reward is likely to replace that slot. Do not hoard forever, but do not upgrade every temporary low-track item immediately.

## 4. Delves, Bountiful keys, Prey, and outdoor progression

### Delves

A Delve is a short role-agnostic scenario for one to five players with Valeera as a configurable companion. It has 11 tiers. Tier 4+ unlocks after an endgame unlock on one character; unlocked tiers carry across Delves.

**Bountiful** Delves have a highlighted entrance and final Coffer. A **Restored Coffer Key**, created from outdoor Coffer Key Shards, opens the better final reward. Ordinary/unkkeyed Delve end loot does not keep scaling past T3, so T4+ runs are normally most valuable when Bountiful and keyed.

| Tier | Bountiful direct chest | World Vault outcome |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–3 | 266 / 269 / 272 Adventurer | 279 / 282 / 285 Veteran |
| 4–6 | 276 Adventurer4 → 282 Veteran2 | 289 Veteran4 → 298 Champion3 |
| 7 | 292 Champion1 | 302 Champion4 |
| 8–11 | 295 Champion2 | 305 Hero1 |

T8 is the major solo-friendly breakpoint: direct rewards cap there and the World Vault reaches 305 Hero1. T9–11 add challenge and stronger crest paths rather than higher direct gear. Use a weekly Delver's/Trovehunter Bounty at a T8+ run you can finish reliably rather than wasting it on a low-tier experiment.

### Prey

Prey is an optional level-90 outdoor hunting overlay. Accept a contract in Silvermoon, do normal outdoor activity to locate the target, then defeat it. Normal/Hard/Nightmare direct gear is 266/279/292; corresponding World Vault potential is 279/292/305. The first two hunts of each difficulty per week are the gear-relevant ones. Extra hunts can be enjoyable, but are not unlimited gear farming.

### Coiled Isle and Vaults

The 12.1 Coiled Isle includes the Vaults of Atal'Utek and public activity chain. Temple Patrols are small solo tasks; Temple Strikes are tougher events that can reward Corrosive Coins, Veteran Mistcrests and a chance at Veteran gear. The Altar of Corrosion power loop begins on the Isle and later benefits wider outdoor/Delve play. Do the introduction once for Warband access; do repeat events only when their rewards or gameplay appeal.

## 5. The Great Vault, explained without the weekly-chore trap

The Great Vault is a **weekly choice window**, not a chest containing every reward. During the current reset week, you unlock offers. After the next reset, choose exactly **one** offer or an alternate reward. It is character-specific: one character’s activity never fills another’s Vault.

| Row | panes unlocked | qualifying activity |
|---|---|---|
| Raid | 2 / 4 / 6 bosses | current raid or Lair bosses |
| Dungeon | 1 / 4 / 8 runs | Heroic, M0, M+, Timewalking |
| World | 2 / 4 / 8 activities | Delves, Prey, zone events, weeklies, Assignments |

More panes mean more alternatives, not more items. The Dungeon row uses your best, fourth-best, and eighth-best qualifying run. One +10 plus seven +2s produces a strong first choice but weaker later choices. World weekly/zone activities count at low World level; a T8+ Delve or Nightmare Prey establishes the 305 Hero1 World ceiling.

If no gear offer is useful, choose Tokens of Merit. Once at least three panes are completed, a **Nebulous Voidcore** alternate choice may be available. It is a bonus-roll chance at eligible raid/Lair, Bountiful Delve, Prey or M+ rewards—not a guaranteed upgrade.

Good weekly behavior: pick one row you enjoy, do the best comfortable activity for it, and add panes only when the additional activity is worthwhile. Filling all nine panes at poor difficulty is not inherently better than one good World or Dungeon option.

## 6. Dungeons and Mythic 0

Normal provides 259 non-upgradeable gear. Heroic provides 276 Adventurer4. Current Season 2 M0 provides 292 Champion1 and resets daily.

M0 is the best bridge from solo content into group play because full mechanics are present but no keystone timer is running. Use it to learn bosses, dangerous trash casts, your interrupt, defensive buttons, and how to follow a tank's route. Gear alone does not teach dungeon knowledge.

## 7. Mythic+: mechanics and a social on-ramp

### Mechanics

Mythic+ (M+) is a five-player dungeon entered with a **keystone**. The keystone selects the dungeon and difficulty. The group tries to complete the dungeon and required Enemy Forces under a timer while handling affixes. It is manually formed; unlike Heroic queueing, there is no automatic M+ matchmaking.

Finish a run even if the timer is missed when the group agrees: an over-time completion can still have end-of-run/Vault value, but it does not earn timed completion rating credit. Exact current depleted-versus-timed loot quantity is not asserted here because current primary documentation does not establish it confidently.

| Key | end-of-run gear | Dungeon Vault |
|---:|---:|---:|
| +2–3 | 295 Champion2 | 305 Hero1 |
| +4 | 298 Champion3 | 308 Hero2 |
| +5 | 302 Champion4 | 308 Hero2 |
| +6 | 305 Hero1 | 311 Hero3 |
| +7 | 305 Hero1 | 315 Hero4 |
| +8–9 | 308 Hero2 | 315 Hero4 |
| +10+ | 311 Hero3 | 318 Myth1 |

At +2–5, **Lindormi's Guidance** marks/weakens relevant enemies, supplies all needed Enemy Forces through those targets, and removes death timer loss. This is deliberately the learning bracket. A rotating Xal'atath's Bargain begins at +5; +7 adds Fortified or Tyrannical; +10 has both; +12 uses Guile instead of Bargains. Do not run above +10 expecting higher item level; higher keys are rating/challenge goals.

### First-key tutorial

1. Learn the dungeon in Heroic/M0 or a low key first.
2. Use your own +2–4 or join a listing explicitly marked learning/completion.
3. Before starting, state that you are returning/learning, ask for route-specific warnings, and agree whether the goal is timing or completion.
4. Stay with the tank. Do not pull extras or start bosses early. Use your interrupt, defensive and utility buttons; survival and control matter more than damage-meter greed.
5. After the run, identify one concrete improvement and repeat. Low-key repetition makes the mode understandable.

### The social tutorial

- **PUG:** pick-up group, strangers for one run.
- **Premade Group Finder:** player-hosted listings; browse/apply or host yourself.
- **Static:** recurring five-player team.
- **Guild/Community:** persistent social home; Communities can be cross-realm.

Hosting your own key is the lowest-pressure way in. Use `I → Premade Groups → Dungeons/Mythic+`, list the actual key and a literal expectation: `+2 [dungeon] | learning / completion | returning player | no voice`. Invite a tank and healer first, then DPS. State the goal before the stone begins. A five-minute wait for people who accept that contract is better than a rushed mismatched group.

When joining, read `push`, `timed`, `fast`, and `farm` as speed-focused language. `completion`, `chill`, `learning`, and `alt` signal lower-pressure intent, but are not a guarantee. A concise application note such as “returning BM Hunter; basic fights reviewed; interrupts ready” is sufficient. Declines are common for DPS applicants and are not a verdict on your worth.

If a run is clearly over time, ask: “Do we want to finish for loot/Vault credit?” Do not assume an over-time run automatically means everyone must continue. Leave calmly if no consensus or if people are abusive. Use Recent Allies/Friends to add kind, competent people after a good PUG and ask whether they are running another; that is how one-off groups become a network.

Voice chat is group policy, not an M+ game requirement. Midnight’s native Boss Warnings, Cooldown Manager, raid frames and ping tools are a good learning baseline. Do not rely on old combat-addon requirements: Midnight limits addons from processing combat-state information to make decisions; presentation/quality-of-life tools can remain useful when current-compatible.

## 8. Raids, LFR, Lairs, tier, and Catalyst

Venomous Abyss is the current eight-boss Season 2 raid. LFR is queueable discovery/training; Normal/Heroic are manually formed flexible raids; Mythic is the stricter coordinated raid format. Modern loot is Need/Greed, not Cataclysm master loot.

Direct raid loot rises by boss band:

| Difficulty | bosses 1 / 2–3 / 4–6 / 7–8 |
|---|---|
| LFR | 279 / 282 / 285 / 289 |
| Normal | 292 / 295 / 298 / 302 |
| Heroic | 305 / 308 / 311 / 315 |
| Mythic | 318 / 321 / 324 / 344 |

**Tier** is a class-set bonus, not merely armour. Helm, shoulders, chest, hands and legs count toward two- and four-piece bonuses. The **Catalyst** converts eligible Season 2 Veteran-or-higher PvE gear (and PvP equivalent) into tier. Crafted armour is not eligible. Use charges to complete a 2- or 4-piece breakpoint rather than converting randomly. 12.1 Catalyst conversion preserves the original secondary/tertiary stats and applicable cantrips.

For a first raid social experience, read each boss’s personal duties, bring food/repair/consumables if expected, announce inexperience before a pull, follow markers and leader calls, and clarify whether the listing is learning, farm, progression or speed run. Exact lockout eligibility is difficulty/current-instance dependent: inspect the in-game Raid tab rather than applying a Cataclysm rule from memory.

## 9. Crafting and professions

Read the full standalone **MIDNIGHT_CRAFTERS_GUIDE.md** alongside this handbook. The essential level-90 interaction is:

- **Spark of Tides** is a character-bound time gate. Most epic crafted gear uses two Sparks; two-handed weapons use four.
- Use a Personal order for important gear; Public orders cannot guarantee quality.
- Agree on exact item, stats, embellishment, materials, minimum quality and commission before submitting.
- The crafter must inspect Expected Quality with the real inputs. A recipe known is not automatically a rank-5 craft.
- You may equip only two embellished items.
- Recraft rather than discard a durable Spark item when changing stats, quality, embellishment or crest/infusion bracket; no new Spark is needed for the same item.

For Virek, Mining is gathering/material support and Engineering is utility/selected gear, not a requirement to self-make every best combat item. Keep them if you enjoy them. Cooking and Fishing are simple secondary professions for food/materials, not an item-level progression gate.

## 10. Warbands and alts

Warband does not mean every character is one character. It is a set of account-wide systems.

| Usually Warband/account benefit | Character-specific |
|---|---|
| Renown/collections, relevant achievements, shared bank access, upgrade-discount achievements, certain marked transferable items/currencies | equipped gear/upgrades, Great Vault, M+ key/rating, raid/dungeon lockouts, Spark use, profession skill/KP/Concentration, most quest progress |

Use an alt’s offered campaign skip/access and clearly transferable items. Do not assume a named currency is transferable; inspect the currency tooltip/UI. An alt still needs its own Vault, Spark plan, tier/Catalyst progress and weekly activity.

Alt stops:

| Stop | Enough work |
|---|---|
| Casual/playable | access unlocks, easy outdoor/Heroic replacements, a few Bountiful Delves |
| Reasonably geared | T7–8 Bountiful path and one desired Vault row; M0/low M+ or LFR/Normal only if wanted |
| Serious | own Vault, tier/Catalyst and Spark plan, then desired raid/M+ progression |

## 11. Finding a guild or recurring social home

Look in the in-game Guild Finder, Raider.IO Recruitment, `r/wowguilds`, current Blizzard recruitment posts, and—most effectively—among people you enjoyed PUGging with. Do not choose based only on the words “active” or “no drama.”

Ask whether the guild has actual low/mid-key activity outside raid night, when it plays in your time zone, whether learner runs exist, whether voice is optional/required for each activity, and how it handles wipes/attendance.

Good signs: concrete schedule, recent sign-ups/group formation, leaders who can explain goals and mistakes calmly, and an invitation to a low-stakes trial run. Warning signs: pressure to transfer/pay/apply before any conversation, contempt for learners, vague claims of “all content” with no recurring activity, or a recruitment post that promises a culture its members do not demonstrate.

Suggested message:

> Hi—I’m a returning Retail player and BM Hunter. I play roughly [days/times/timezone] and want a friendly place to learn Delves, M0 and gradually +2–5 keys. I prepare and learn, but I’m not looking for a high-pressure push group. Do you have people who run that content outside raid nights, and could I join a low-stakes run before deciding?

Try multiple groups. A good guild should make you want to log in, not feel as though you accepted a second job.

## 12. First-character and alt routes

### First/main character

1. Finish campaign and Coiled Isle/Vault introductions.
2. Replace obvious leveling holes through outdoor content, Heroics and comfortable Bountiful Delves.
3. Establish one enjoyable World Vault option; use T8 only when reliable and enjoyable.
4. Try LFR/Normal, M0 or +2–5 for learning—not because you have to race there.
5. Use a Spark only after identifying a durable weak slot or desired embellishment.
6. Move into Normal/Heroic raid or +6–10 if that content is fun and your group goals support it.

### Common resource traps

- Using Season 1/12.0.5 reward tables.
- Spending a Bounty below T8.
- Using Public orders for quality-critical gear.
- Upgrading temporary low-track items shortly before a known replacement.
- Filling eight poor Vault activities only to say the Vault is full.
- Treating Delve item level as proof of dungeon/raid encounter knowledge.
- Assuming Warband-wide means Vault/Spark/KP progress is shared.
- Building a social plan around an obsolete combat addon or a speed-PUG culture you do not actually enjoy.

## 13. Decision framework

1. Is a system unlock or weak slot stopping the content I want? Solve that first.
2. Which single weekly Vault row is enjoyable and valuable this week? Do that at the best comfortable tier.
3. Does an activity give a direct upgrade, tier progress, suitable Mistcrests, useful practice, or fun? If none, skip it.
4. Before spending crests/Sparks, compare against known next rewards and free same-slot catch-up.
5. Stop at the chosen main/alt stopping point. Maximum item level is a choice, not the default obligation.
