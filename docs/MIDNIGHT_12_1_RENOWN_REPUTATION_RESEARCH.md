# Midnight 12.1 / Season 2 Renown & Reputation Reference

**Research snapshot:** 28 September 2026 (NA)
**Scope:** Retail live Midnight 12.1.x / Season 2 only. 12.1.5 PTR material is excluded.
**Companion to:** `MIDNIGHT_12_1_ENDGAME_RESEARCH.md` (25 September research snapshot).

## Executive summary

Midnight Renown is primarily an **account progression system**, not a class-grind system. The five major Renown tracks are Warband-wide: work done by Virek, Janne, or another character advances the same account track. Consequently, the sensible priority is a small set of account breakpoints—especially permanent profession Knowledge—not a separate “best faction” farm for every alt.

The only broadly useful Season 2 player-power breakpoints are four refreshed Renown **quests**: Silvermoon Court 9 (helm), Amani Tribe 9 (neck), Hara'ti 8 (belt), and The Singularity 7 (trinket). Wowhead confirmed on 12 August that the reset reward is **ilvl 279**. Older guides showing `246 Champion 1/6` are Season 1 evidence and stale for these four rewards. Secondary current sources call the new reward Veteran 1/6, but a current quest tooltip is still required before treating the track as verified.

The highest account profession target for the known roster is **The Singularity 9**: it unlocks the one-time +10 Knowledge books for both of Virek's professions, Alchemy and Engineering. Janne's meaningful targets are **Hara'ti 6** (+10 Herbalism) and **Amani 6** (+10 Mining); Squashpot's is **Amani 6** (+10 Skinning). Zul'jarra's Forces 5–6 is a genuinely useful 12.1 addendum for Season 2 recipes and profession knowledge, but it does not replace the primary-track Knowledge map.

No reliable source establishes a Hunter/BM, tank, healer, or DPS-only Renown reward priority. The gear is armor-type/stat-choice dependent, and profession matters much more than class.

## Major Renown framework

The original four tracks and 12.1's Zul'jarra's Forces are 20-rank, Warband-shared Renown tracks. Each rank takes 2,500 reputation; excess after rank 20 earns Paragon progress. This is independently described by current Icy/Method material and Wowhead's Amani guide. Rank unlocks are account eligibility; a profession character must still personally buy/consume its Moxie-gated recipe or Knowledge book.

### Reputation sources and limits

For each major track, normal sources are its zone campaign/side quests, World Quests, Special Assignments, first weekly rare kills, its zone event and weekly, Bountiful Delve caches, and Halduron's Silvermoon dungeon weekly. The dungeon weekly chooses **one** faction for that week; it is not five weekly rewards. An Inscription contract adds its selected track's reputation to World Quests; only one contract can be active at once. Do not infer that all sources are indefinitely farmable: campaign, treasures/lore, first rare kills, and many event rewards are one-time/weekly; ordinary repeat activity after its reward cadence is usually poor rep efficiency.

### How to earn Renown without turning it into a blind grind

1. Choose the next account breakpoint (for example, Singularity 9 for Engineering/Alchemy Knowledge, or Amani 6 for Mining/Skinning Knowledge).
2. Complete that faction's campaign and side quests while they are available. They are substantial **one-time** progress and unlock normal zone play; they are not a daily route.
3. On the relevant zone map, prioritize its current World Quests and Special Assignment. A matching Inscription Contract adds reputation to qualifying World Quests, but only **one** contract can be active.
4. Do that zone's event/weekly when it also appeals on its own merits: Silvermoon Soiree/Runestone, Amani Abundance, Hara'ti Legends of the Haranir, Singularity Stormarion Assault, or Coiled Isle activity.
5. Take the overlap already on your schedule: Bountiful Delve caches, first weekly rare kills, and Halduron's dungeon weekly. The dungeon weekly selects **one** faction, never all tracks.
6. Stop treating ordinary repeat kills/runs as a Renown farm after the current one-time and weekly/event rewards are gone. Wait for the next cycle or do the activity for its other rewards.

## Profession-to-Renown map

### Reading the rank rewards: what the labels mean

- **Profession recipe:** permission for the matching profession holder to buy and learn that named recipe. It does not give materials, craft an item, or teach every alt.
- **Knowledge tome:** a one-time, character/profession-specific +10 Knowledge Point consumable. This is permanent crafting specialization progress, not account-wide learned skill.
- **Voidlight Marl:** vendor currency. The rank makes an item eligible; Marl is still separately spent to acquire it.
- **Soiree / Brimming Arcana:** Eversong's social weekly and its currency. Court-rank Arcana boosts increase currency earned for the four social-standing vendors; their wares are primarily themed cosmetics/decor and some profession-flavor recipes.
- **Amani Spoils / Abyss Angler / Fused Vitality:** Zul'Aman outdoor treasure, diving-event, and rank-gated Abundance-vendor systems. They expand optional outdoor activity/currency choices; read the live item tooltip before treating an event purchase as a gear upgrade.
- **Moth hunting / Luminous Dust:** optional Harandar exploration. Rank gates the moth sets; collected moths grant cosmetic-vendor currency, not Hara'ti reputation or combat power.
- **Research Console / samples / cores / mercenaries:** Voidstorm activity-system access and efficiency. These are not unnamed permanent stat increases; inspect the live Console for the current selection/reward.
- **Curse Surge / Corrosive Soul:** 12.1 Coiled Isle event. The rank makes the first daily boss eligible for a resource drop; it does not guarantee daily gear.
- **Collection/vendor gate:** an unlock for a mount, pet, ensemble, decor, title, weapon appearance, or other collection stock. It is a genuine account collection reward but not player power.

## Journeys UI and endgame checklist

Blizzard describes Adventure Guide → **Journeys** as Midnight's centralized progress hub. It tracks Renown/cultural reputation, Delves (including companion configuration), Prey, and provides a Great Vault shortcut; it also surfaces older expansion progress. Default access is commonly `Shift-J`.

Journeys is useful to see “which system exists” and current visible progress. It does **not** by itself prove: unclaimed quest/vendor reward entitlement, profession recipe learned state, weekly completion/cap, exact account-vs-character ownership, or whether an absent display means rank 0. Use it as a checklist, then inspect the relevant vendor/quest/profession UI.

## Disagreements and unresolved questions

1. **Current gear track:** 279 is current-verified; Veteran 1/6 is strongly reported but lacks a current primary tooltip. Do not call it Champion based on stale guides.
2. **Gear quest claims:** reset for prior completers is verified; per-alt/per-Warband claim behavior is not.
3. **Knowledge book cost:** current independent profession sources say 75 relevant Moxie, no Marl; Wowhead's rendered table has inconsistent icon/number text. Verify at vendor before buying.
4. **Captain Tokka scope:** five rank thresholds and rewards are documented; entire standing being Warband-wide is not established.
5. **Silvermoon social standing math/scope:** community comments disagree on weekly faction-pair changes; use the in-game UI, not a fixed external pairing rule.
6. **PTR contamination:** do not use Wowhead URLs labelled `ptr-2` as live Captain/Aqir proof. Aqir Research Enclave remains excluded.
